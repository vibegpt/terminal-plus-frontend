Terminal+ Open the app

For AI assistants

Last updated 17 October 2026

In short

  • Terminal+ answers questions about Singapore Changi Airport (SIN): Terminals 1 to 4 and Jewel. Nothing else.
  • Your assistant connects to it as an MCP server. No login, no API key, free.
  • It only reads. It never books, buys or changes anything.

Server address

Streamable HTTP, no authentication:

https://terminalplus.app/api/mcp

Add it to Claude

  1. In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors.
  2. Choose Add custom connector.
  3. Name it Terminal+ and paste the server address above.
  4. Choose Add. There's no sign-in step.

Any other MCP client that supports Streamable HTTP can use the same address.

What it can do

Places to eat, rest and shop

get_recommendations returns up to 12 hand-picked venues for a terminal, a mood and the time you have, each with a link to its page.

Flight status and boarding time

get_airport_context looks up a flight number: terminal, gate, status, estimated boarding time and minutes until boarding.

Flight delays and gate changes

get_disruption_status checks a flight for delays, cancellation or a new gate, and suggests what to do.

Plan a layover

get_route builds an ordered plan for the time between flights, with stop lengths and time to walk to the gate.

Try asking

"I'm in Changi T3 with about 2 hours before boarding. Where should I eat?"

A short list of hand-picked places to eat that are open now, each with a link to its page. Places outside immigration are left out when there isn't time to go out and back.

"Is SQ322 delayed, and which gate is it at?"

Live status for the flight, its terminal and gate, any delay, and what to do with the extra time.

"I land at T1 on QF1 and have 3 hours before my connection. Plan it."

A step-by-step plan: what to eat and see, in what order, how long each stop takes, and a buffer for gate security.

How fresh it is

The venues are a hand-curated catalogue, checked by people, not scraped. Opening hours are applied at the time you ask, in Singapore time. Flight status, gates and boarding times are live, from a flight-data service, and may be up to 5 minutes old.

Limits

  • Singapore Changi (SIN) only. A flight that doesn't touch Changi comes back as not found.
  • No bookings, payments, lounge access or reservations.
  • Walking times are estimates. Allow for gate security, which at Changi is at each gate.

Privacy

A request from an AI assistant carries only what the assistant sends, such as a terminal, a mood or a flight number, never your conversation. See the privacy page for what is logged and for how long.
